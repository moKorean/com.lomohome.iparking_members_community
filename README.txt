For residents of a building that uses iParking: register parking for visitors coming to your own home, through iParking MEMBERS. Sign in once, and the device shows how many vehicles are expected today, tomorrow and this week, how many are parked inside right now, and when the next visit is due. Register a plate and a date from the settings page or a Flow action, give frequent visitors a one-press button, and review or cancel three months of registrations either side of today.

This is an unofficial, community-built app and is not affiliated with iParking. You need your own unit's account at a building enrolled in iParking MEMBERS, and a registration takes effect on your building's real access-control system immediately — so sign in with your own credentials and register only vehicles visiting you. It speaks an API iParking has never published, so the vendor can change it and break this app without notice, and that server downgrades every secure request to a plain one, which means the access token and plate data travel unencrypted after sign-in. Signing in itself uses a verified secure connection, your credentials never leave your own device except to iParking's servers, and the app performs no analytics.
